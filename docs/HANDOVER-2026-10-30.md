DualBladeX is a Valorant streamer on Twitch and YouTube. Their stack is custom: two physical machines, several
codebases, and all of it automated.

- **Public URL:** `https://hub.dualbladex.org`, reached through a Cloudflare tunnel that ends at the Mac Mini.
- **Main docs:** `/home/aryan/projects/CLAUDE.md` is the architecture and every hard-won constraint; read it first. Specs and plans are in `/home/aryan/projects/docs/superpowers/` (outside every repo; a copy is pushed on the `stream-backend` branch `later-fixes` under `docs/editor-app/`).

### What exists, in brief
- **Stream features (backend `stream/`):**
  - Streamer.bot WebSocket client: chat from Twitch and YouTube, with chat replies.
  - Channel-point roulette, with weapon votes and an affordability filter.
  - Credit OCR: the gaming PC sends buy-menu screenshots; the Mac Mini runs Tesseract on them.
  - Points through Streamlabs Cloudbot: it is driven by chat commands, because Cloudbot has no API.
  - Spotify song requests, `!rank`, a first-time-chatter greeter, Discord go-live posts.
  - The break timer, and widget fan-out over `/ws/widgets`.
- **Core (backend `core/`):**
  - Config with a schema (`config_schema.FIELDS`) and a Settings page that masks secrets.
  - GitHub OAuth login with two roles: admin (the streamer) and editor (the hired video editor).
  - Sessions in SQLite (`app.db`).
  - The scheduler, with a clock-sanity gate, because the Mac Mini loses power and its clock.
- **Editor app (backend `editor/` + site `/editor`):** for handing stream VODs to a hired video editor and reviewing cuts.
  - Google Drive storage (`drive.file` scope): large files never pass through the Mac Mini.
  - Projects, tapes and versions; two-step deletes with an hour of undo.
  - Discord DMs, a notifications bell, comments and threads, the review player.
- **Gaming PC:** `agent.py` presses B to capture the buy-menu credit value, and `uploader.py` handles the right-click "Send to editor" upload to Drive.
